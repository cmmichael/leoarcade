Walk with the arrow keys, or tap the desert to walk there. Space shoots and spars, G ghost hands, M math, B belt, S supernova, D dance, L lightning.

How to play

  • Beat the pyramid ghosts, 20 levels. Ghosts hide inside pyramids and pop out to chase you. You can only hit them when they're outside. Every level, the ghosts get tougher and faster, and more pyramids appear. The game remembers the highest level you've reached.
  • Power boost (M). Solve a math problem to get one power bar, up to 5. Each shot does as much damage as your power bars, so more math means stronger blasts. With zero power, your shots can't hurt ghosts. Getting hit costs a heart and a power bar. The math gets harder at levels 6, 11 and 16.
  • Prism shots bounce off the walls one time, so you can bank them around corners.
  • Spar to reflect. Every Space press also swings a sparring ring for a moment. Ghost blobs that touch it bounce back at the ghosts, and a ghost you spar gets knocked away without hurting you.
  • Supernova (S). A giant blast that hits every ghost that's out. It needs at least one power bar and takes 15 seconds to recharge. With the belt off it's even stronger, but it cracks every pyramid and fills the Unleashed meter fast.
  • Dance (D). Prism Paradox dances and the whole floor lights up. It starts as a rainbow, then keeps changing into new repeating color patterns. While he dances, every ghost and its attacks freeze, so it's the perfect time to shoot. Dance lasts 8 seconds and recharges in 20.
  • Lightning (L). Lightning needs bolts, and you charge each bolt by solving a math problem (you can hold 3). With a bolt ready, press L and a target swings across the closest ghost. Press L again to strike. A hit on the red heart does 10 damage, which strikes out early ghosts instantly. Later ghosts need two or three heart hits. Anywhere else on the ghost does 4. Tip: dance first so the ghost holds still.
  • Ghost hands give you a chance to spawn a ghost twin that looks just like you. It floats in ghost form, then switches back to its normal form and fights when an enemy appears.
  • Power safe belt (B). With the belt on, Prism Paradox is just normal. With it off: every shot is bigger and does +1 damage, Supernova does +3, and every few seconds he might turn invincible for 4 seconds (no damage, even stronger shots). The catch: his power leaks out and fills the Unleashed meter, and his shots crack pyramids. Three cracks break a pyramid, so that ghost can never hide again. If the meter fills, the universe starts breaking and you must solve a problem to fix it. Then the belt snaps back on. Putting the belt back on drains the meter.
