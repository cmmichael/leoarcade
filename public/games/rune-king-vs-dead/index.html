How to play
  • Each room is a big 3D model you look down into. Drag the round stick to walk anywhere. Press SMASH (or tap the screen) to throw your hammer at the closest monster.
  • Each monster shows its health number. Every smash takes some away.
  • Press MATH and answer a question. Your answer becomes your hammer power for 3 smashes!
  • Chapter 2: the upgraded Rune King has wings, six clawed hands and laser eyes. LASER zaps from far away and is the only thing that breaks the dragon’s shield. CLAWS hit every monster close to you. In Chapter 2 your hammer power is your answer times 10.
  • In a tight spot? Press POOF (or T) to teleport a little way to the safest spot nearby. It takes a moment to recharge.
  • Bats are fast. Big slimes split in two. Spiders drop from the roof. Skeletons throw bones, so walk out of the way!
  • Find all 5 triangle runes (green, blue, yellow, orange and red) and your pet dragon flies in. It breathes fire at the monsters by itself!
  • There are 4 special runes too: Star, Water, Shrink and Giant.
  • White arrows at the edge of the screen point to monsters you can’t see. Coloured arrows point to runes.
  • Monsters drop runes. Your runes show up at the bottom of the screen. Tap one to wear it around your waist:
